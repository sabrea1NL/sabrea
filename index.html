Our Story

Made by hand, worn for a lifetime.

sabrea.nl began at a small workbench in the Netherlands, where every piece was — and still is — shaped, set and polished by hand. We work with recycled gold and ethically sourced stones, in runs small enough that a single jeweler sees a piece from sketch to box.

We don't chase trends. We build quiet, considered pieces meant to be worn daily and passed down eventually — jewelry as an heirloom, not an impulse.
